# Parts-Live-Archive

Her words for this repo: "the finished parts that we're actually using... the stars that made it out of the pile."

Each folder is one finished part that has been installed into main-assembly in the December repo. A part comes here only after it was fixed up, proven to work, installed, and a handoff was written in the same session. Her words: "that session isn't over until a handoff is written, documenting that the piece has been installed, and then the part itself is moved out of part assembly into parts archive."

In each part's folder:

- `HANDOFF.md`: the installation handoff, the same file as in main-assembly: what it is, where it came from, how it was proven, where it plugs in.
- `INSTALL.patch`: exactly what the part changed in main-assembly, so it can be read or applied again.
- `files/`: the part's files as they stand in main-assembly right after it was installed, at their real paths.

Parts are copied here. The originals in the storage repos (pre-dec, Beyond-October and the others) are left where they are.
